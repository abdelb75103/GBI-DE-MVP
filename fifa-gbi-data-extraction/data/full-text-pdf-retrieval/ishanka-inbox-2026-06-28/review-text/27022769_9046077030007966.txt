Résultats. – Une association signiﬁcative était retrouvée entre l'incidence des blessures et la

compared to injuries deﬁned by an inability to play a match or an inability to take part in more than 3 days of training, collected by
the club doctor during the season.
Results. – There was a signiﬁcant association between injuries and week-to-week changes in load. For each new spike, injuries

Conclusion. – This study showed signiﬁcant association between week-to-week changes in load and injury incidence, which was
supported by scientiﬁc literature and encouraged its use in injury prevention.
