09Find out which conclusions survive reasonable changes0 / 4
  • Run the agreed alternative assumptions
    WhatRepeat the planned checks with alternative priors, sharing assumptions and evidence restrictions.
    WhyA headline may depend on one fragile choice.
    HowUse the same targets and report both the conclusion change and evidence lost. Include follow-up and study-quality restrictions where evaluable.
  • Compare settings only where they genuinely align
    WhatCheck overall results against their complete match/training components.
    WhyDifferent populations or omitted settings can create misleading discrepancies.
    HowUse the same population, period and definition. Include all relevant settings or label the check inapplicable; do not treat separate model runs as a joint probability calculation.
  • Test prediction without giving away the answer
    WhatTest the kind of prediction we intend to report.
    WhyRetaining related records can make a model look better than it is.
    HowFor a new study, hold out its whole independent source family. Compare models on the same observations and report how uncertain the comparison is.
  • Add secondary results only where supported
    WhatExamine category patterns and linked frequency/severity if eligible.
    WhyThese should explain the main results without adding unsupported claims.
    HowCheck completeness and comparability first, then use the pre-specified models and validations. Keep incomplete category reporting descriptive.
