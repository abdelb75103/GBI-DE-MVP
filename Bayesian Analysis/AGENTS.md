# FIFA GBI Bayesian analysis

- Read `CURRENT_PROTOCOL.md` for the primary protocol, adjudicated decisions and current human gate before continuing methodology or analysis work.

- Treat `fifa-gbi-bayesian-learning-packet/` as a learning resource, not an approved final analysis plan.
- Before analysis or methodology work, read its `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `DECISION_CHECKLIST.md`, and `SCOPING_METHODS.md`.
- Preserve the evidence trail in `SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`, `CORRESPONDENCE_LOG.md`, and `MANIFEST.md`. A bounded scoping finding is not proof that no contrary study exists.
- A later method supersedes an earlier proposal only when the approved decision and rationale are recorded in the methodology or decision record used for that analysis.
- Reproducible analysis retains the exact input/row version, code, seeds, software/package/compiler versions, formal model and priors, diagnostics, predictive checks, sensitivity analyses, and linked outputs. Follow `LEARNING_GUIDE.md` section 12.
