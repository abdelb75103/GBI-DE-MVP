# Source-alignment decisions

`alignment-decisions.csv` is a row-complete adjudication of the 3,211 observations in `injury-analysis-master.csv`. It is a source-alignment record. It does not freeze an analysis set, resolve cohort dependence, or authorise model fitting.

The required exporter fields are the first nine columns: observation ID; preserved outcome descriptor, location, type and diagnosis; threshold route; metric-alignment status; reason; and evidence. Extra fields retain the numerical route, evidence grade, restricted-scope decision, matched-pair decision, title and exact time-loss wording.

`metric_alignment_status=supported` means the saved evidence supports a time-loss/player-hour rate for the stated scope. It does not state a likelihood. `metric_usability=count_hour` has a retained aligned count and player-hour denominator. `rate_ci` has a retained rate and confidence interval. `descriptive_only` has an aligned reported rate but neither of those numerical inputs. It is still an aligned metric and must not be treated as a count likelihood. `approximate_count_hour_sensitivity` identifies a count and rounded crude rate that support an approximate reverse exposure only in sensitivity work. `none` identifies a source-level metric problem, such as an unresolved numerator/denominator conflict, a non-player-hour denominator, an absent exposure unit, or an explicitly non-defensible rate. It is not assigned just because a count or confidence interval is missing.

`threshold_route=main` applies to explicit or source-audited positive-day restrictions. `sensitivity` applies to otherwise aligned time-loss metrics whose threshold remains unclear. `hold` applies only when a separable time-loss outcome is absent or another stated rule prevents use. `current_session_only_not_ruled_out` is not an exclusion: it remains in the threshold sensitivity route. An explicit current-session-only definition would be held.

The 120 matching overall/category pairs are in `matching-overall-category-adjudication.csv`. Their matching numeric rate is never evidence of common scope. A title/definition-confirmed pair establishes one restricted clinical outcome, so every verified parent and specialist alias receives the same narrow canonical `outcome_scope`, with available location, type and diagnosis retained alongside it. It does not by itself create a count. Where the frozen source record has a directly extracted count and player-hour denominator for that same verified outcome, the parent retains `count_hour` support rather than losing sampling information to descriptive aliases. The 30 bounded parent records in `review/same-outcome-information-loss.csv` are reconciled this way. Direct burden evidence remains source-aligned and descriptive for the owner numerical route because no days-lost likelihood is specified. A study-wide sole time-loss definition applies to its reported anatomy and tissue rates unless the source context records a conflicting quantity or mixed definition. A mixed medical-attention or complaint definition needs an explicit time-loss subset for a detailed rate.

`outcome_scope` is the final canonical scope where a source-confirmed restricted scope exists, including a metric hold. Holds without such source confirmation retain the unresolved source descriptor. `raw_outcome_scope` retains the original value. `outcome-scope-map.csv` records the stable mapping, including the source-specific restricted scopes for S300, S2823 and S2040.

`reverse-exposure-review.csv` records the bounded review of 204 pre-existing reverse-arithmetic candidates and seven S417 rates. `reverse_exposure_supported=true` requires a retained observed integer count, a same-setting crude per-1,000-hour rate, compatible unit, time-loss definition and no source metric conflict. Its inferred exposure is approximate, sensitivity-only and never sets `model_eligibility` to true. S417 meets this evidence test. The source audit confirms injury-frequency rates, not adjusted rates, and says that rate inversion must retain rounding uncertainty. S156 is held because its retained count, exposure and reported rate disagree.

`verified_injury_count` and `count_alignment_evidence` retain source-audited counts that the frozen master does not place in its generic count cell. They preserve, rather than alter, the frozen input. S2147 has a primary-source-confirmed 611-injury time-loss subset with 143,573 football hours; the Methods exclude ten non-football injuries before analysis, so those ten must not be subtracted from the 611 subset. `reported_rate_alignment`, `reported_time_loss_rate_per_1000`, `calculated_crude_count_rate_per_1000` and `published_rate_method` flag that its printed 4.2 per 1,000 h is a GLMM estimate, whereas 611/143,573 is a crude 4.2557 per 1,000 h. The row remains a threshold sensitivity incidence observation. Its imputed days-lost burden remains held. S223 remains held because its extracted time-loss-labelled count has no saved source-context link to a time-loss definition. S300 retains its corrected 312 match injuries and 12,507 player-hours.

Known estimated or imputed days-lost burden is held. The approval of the eight named exposure constructions is recorded as evidence, but it does not override a time-loss, metric, scope or days-lost hold. The 12 September primary-source audit takes precedence for its named studies.

`validation.json` records the 3,211 unique IDs, 120 pair reviews, output checksum and route counts. Frozen inputs and project code were not changed.
